The product

Inventory tracking that stays current as your team works.

  • Track usage

    Record inventory activity as supplies are used, so counts reflect what is actually on hand.

  • See stock and expiration status

    Review quantities by item and location, and identify low, depleted, or expiring inventory.

  • Plan replenishment

    Use recorded usage history to help estimate when items may need to be reordered.
